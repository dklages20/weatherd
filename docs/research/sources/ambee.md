# Ambee

- **Coverage:** Commercial environmental-data platform; pollen, air quality, weather, fire, and more.
- **Use it for:** Pollen and environmental data. Its documentation lists current, historical, and forecast endpoints for weather, air quality, and pollen.
- **Pricing:** Sales-led / quote-based. Do not model it as a free API without a written plan entitlement.

## APIs

- [API overview](https://docs.ambeedata.com/apis/overview)
- [Weather API](https://docs.ambeedata.com/apis/weather)
- [Air Quality API](https://docs.ambeedata.com/apis/air-quality)
- [Pollen API](https://docs.ambeedata.com/apis/pollen)
- [API keys / authentication](https://docs.ambeedata.com/apis/overview#api-keys)

## Caveats

Confirm geographic availability, forecast horizon, species resolution, update cadence, redistribution rights, and pricing directly with Ambee. Those details are material for an allergy-focused product.
